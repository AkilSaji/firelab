# Code of conduct

FireLab is for anyone who makes apps and games for TVs, whatever their
experience. We want working on it to feel welcoming and safe.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and
not the person, help newcomers, and credit other people's work.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexual, racist or otherwise
discriminatory remarks; threaten people; publish someone's private information;
or keep going after someone asks you to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent
the project.

**If something goes wrong**, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability" also reaches us for conduct
reports) or by the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. We read every
report, keep it confidential, and may warn, remove content from, or ban anyone
who breaks these rules.
